About ConcertTrack

What this app is, how it works, and why it was built.

What is ConcertTrack?

ConcertTrack is a web application that helps music fans discover and organize upcoming live events. Instead of scrolling through a cluttered ticketing site, users can search for concerts by artist name or city, browse clean event cards, and save events they're interested in to a personal watchlist.

Who is it for?

College students and young adults who regularly attend live events but struggle to keep track of upcoming shows across multiple artists and cities. ConcertTrack brings everything into one focused, distraction-free interface.

The Information Problem

Concert-goers often discover an event, intend to follow up on it, and then lose track of it entirely. ConcertTrack solves this by letting users save events immediately to a personal watchlist, creating a central reference without requiring any account creation or login.
